## Complete tested control

`ridge_ownership.py` samples each original curve at ≤1 voxel, builds transverse probability-logit + CT-DoG basins in a 4-voxel search disk, and links maxima by mutual-nearest world geometry. The final smoothed centers supply owner runs. A certificate requires two separate bright basins, sustained owner runs of at least 8 voxels, at least 8 voxels of simultaneous competition, and both tube lifetimes covering the transition plus 8 voxels on each side.

A certified curve returns to its complete original MLP geometry before the same native cleanup. IDs, point count, order, and gaps are preserved. The whole-curve fallback avoids creating short fragments that immediately fail the native 100-voxel minimum. The original centering increment is retained for every other curve.

Synthetic checks exercise two persistent ridges, reversed traversal, one owner, one peak, flat CT, and ridge death followed by a continuing single peak. The dying-ridge fixture exposed the original short-lifetime certificate; the final lifetime context fixes that mechanism.

**Development result: 0 certified fallbacks among 654 raw curves across the two already revealed cubes.** The control leaves their geometry unchanged. Marker-identity guard expansion is stopped at this operating point. The original CT method keeps its previously frozen matched-coverage results, including the raw regression and radius3 sensitivity.
